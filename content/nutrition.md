1–1.5 lb/week at ~250 lb, and the protein is what protects muscle while the
weight comes off.

## Fasting rules of engagement

- Outside the window: water, black coffee, tea, zero-cal drinks. Inside:
  the plan above.
- Morning walks and core work fasted are fine — that's easy-pace work. If a
  strength session feels flat, slide it later, closer to the window.
- Feeling lightheaded isn't a badge, it's data: drink water, salt your
  food, and if it persists — eat. **The fast serves the deficit; the
  deficit doesn't serve the fast.**
- Weigh in mornings after the bathroom, before coffee. Log it with your
  coach — the chart does the judging, not the mirror.

## Why this setup holds up

- Calorie-matched, [intermittent fasting and continuous dieting lose the
  same weight](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11930668/) — and
  [16:8 shows the best adherence of the fasting
  protocols](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9099935/).
  Adherence is the whole game; this is the pattern you already live.
- Fasting's one real risk while cutting is muscle. The fix is what this
  plan does: [big per-meal protein doses](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5828430/)
  across only 2–3 feedings, plus the strength work in the weekly plan.
- Macros in the planner are per-serving estimates — close enough to steer
  by. The weigh-in trend is the real feedback loop; when it drifts, the
  coach adjusts the calorie target, not the effort.
