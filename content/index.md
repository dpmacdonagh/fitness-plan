title: The Plan
---

**Goal:** go from 250 lb and sedentary to ready for an electrical or plumbing apprenticeship in 2027.

## Targets

- **End of 2026:** roughly 225–230 lb, losing 1–1.5 lb per week, with consistent conditioning built up along the way.
- **Long-term dream:** bike across America. That means building an aerobic base now — elliptical to start, converting to a stationary bike around October.

## Nutrition

- Moderate calorie deficit — steady, not drastic.
- 150–180 g of protein per day.

## Philosophy

Consistency beats intensity. Month one is deliberately easy — the point is to show up, not to suffer. Every completed easy workout is a checkmark that compounds.

Start with the [training rules](/rules/), then check off days in the [weekly logs](/weeks/).
